# WindCredit Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

WindCredit helps Palm Beach County homeowners estimate hurricane-insurance (wind-mitigation) discounts. It is built to keep your information on your phone.

**What stays on your phone.** Your address, your answers about your home, the numbers from your insurance policy, your plan and your reminders are stored only on your device. Photos of your declarations page are read on the device by Apple's built-in text recognition and are never uploaded or saved by WindCredit.

**What we collect.** Nothing. WindCredit has no servers and does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

**Public records lookups.** To find your home, the app sends the address you type to public government services: Palm Beach County's address and parcel services, the Palm Beach County Property Appraiser website, and the Florida statewide parcel service. These are the same records anyone can look up. Apple Maps suggests addresses as you type.

**Google sign-in (optional).** If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name and email with the app so they can appear on the report you send to your insurance agent. They stay on your phone. Deleting your data in Settings also removes WindCredit's access to your Google account. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

**Deleting your data.** Settings ▸ Delete my data erases everything WindCredit stored and cancels reminders. Deleting the app does the same.

**Children.** WindCredit is meant for homeowners and isn't directed at children under 13.
