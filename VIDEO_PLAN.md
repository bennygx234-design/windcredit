## What the research says wins (applied below)

| Pattern | Evidence | How this video uses it |
|---|---|---|
| Use almost all 3 minutes, never go over | 7 winning 2025 videos ran 2:50–3:03 | Target 2:50 |
| Open on the problem, not your name | Most 2025 winners opened with a fact or story (OptiSense, LeftoSense, RoadWatch) | First words: "$6,323" |
| App on screen early; the demo is 40–60% of the video | LeftoSense, RoadWatch | Demo starts 0:30, runs 65 seconds |
| Your face only at the start and end | RoadWatch about 15% face | 4 face clips, about 40 seconds total |
| Explain code by showing it and saying *why* | Delta (real code on screen), Computerpreter ("we chose X because…") | Diagram, then 2 real lines of Swift, then one "because" |
| Name one hard problem and how you fixed it | Judges also read this as a written question | "Discounts don't add up" |
| Outside proof boosts Impact | OnSight (teacher on camera), Computerpreter (expert quote) | A real Boca home and the neighborhood data; add a person if you can |
| Many judges watch muted | Apple app-preview guidance | Every number gets an on-screen caption |
| Rubric: Code scores 1 if "video does not explain code"; Structure 1 if confusing or over time | Official rubric | Both handled explicitly |

## Your scripts

These lines are drafts. Change any word that doesn't sound like you; judges notice memorized robot-speak. Keep the numbers exactly as written: they match the app and its sources.

### YOU-1: Hook (0:00–0:10, about 25 words)
*Outside, phone on a tripod or held steady at eye level, house behind you, sun behind the camera.*

> "In Palm Beach County, the average home insurance bill is **six thousand three hundred and twenty-three dollars** a year. And most of us are paying for discounts we never claimed."

### YOU-2: Who and what (0:13–0:30, about 45 words)

> "I'm **[full name]**, a **[grade]** at **[school]**. This is **WindCredit**, an iPhone app that shows Florida homeowners which hurricane-insurance discounts their home qualifies for but isn't getting, in dollars. I built it for families here in Palm Beach County, especially in older homes."

The bold sentence is your required one-sentence purpose. Say it slowly.

### VO: Voiceover over the app footage
*Record in a quiet room (a closet works), phone 6 inches from your mouth. Read each line; leave a breath between lines so they're easy to cut.*

- **VO-1 (0:30):** "Florida law says storm-strong homes pay less. A hip roof alone can cut the hurricane part of your bill by almost half. But you have to prove it, and most people don't know they qualify."
- **VO-2 (0:40):** "So you type your address, and WindCredit pulls your home straight from Palm Beach County's public records: year built, roof type, homestead."
- **VO-3 (0:50):** "Then four questions, with pictures, so anyone can answer. And 'not sure' is fine."
- **VO-4 (1:00):** "Snap your insurance page. It's read right on the phone. Nothing gets uploaded."
- **VO-5 (1:07):** "And here's the answer. This home is likely missing **thirteen hundred to two thousand dollars a year**, and a hundred-fifty-dollar inspection pays for itself in about six weeks." Then **stop talking for 2 seconds** and let the number sit.
- **VO-6 (1:20):** "Then it gives you a plan, step by step. And it zooms out: **96 percent** of the homes around this one were built before Florida's 2002 building code. One tap sends that to your representative."
- **VO-7 (1:35):** "Here's how it works. County records, then your policy, then Florida's official credit table, then dollars."
- **VO-9 (2:05):** "I built it in Swift and SwiftUI, with Apple's Vision framework for reading the page and MapKit for addresses."

### VO-8 / YOU-SCREEN: The code (1:45–2:05). **Write this in your own words**
This is the rubric's Code row, and the rules require that you understand the code. Screen-record Xcode (QuickTime ▸ New Screen Recording) while you talk.

What to show, in order:
1. `Services/DiscountEngine.swift`, **line 63** (the `let key = …` line)
2. `Services/DiscountEngine.swift`, **line 144** (`let missed = …`)

The idea you must get across (say it your way):
- Florida's discounts **don't add up**. Hip roof is 47%, clips are 35%, but together they're 62%, not 82%. If you add them you get impossible numbers.
- So instead of adding, the code **builds a key out of every feature** (line 63) and **looks up that exact combination** in the state's official table, which I converted into JSON.
- Then line 144 takes the gap between the credit you could get and the one you get now, and turns it into dollars.

One "because" sentence judges love: *"I made the range cautious, 50 to 75 percent of the table's number, because each insurer's table is a little different, and I didn't want to promise money that isn't there."*

### YOU-3: Why it matters (2:15–2:35, about 50 words)
Pick ONE version. **Only say what's true.**

- **A, if you tested real homes:** "I tested WindCredit on **[N]** homes in Boca. In **[X]** of them, it found a discount the family wasn't getting." (A 5-second clip of a neighbor or parent reacting makes this much stronger.)
- **B, if you haven't tested yet:** "**[Your real reason, e.g. 'My family's premium went up again this year']**. Florida law already requires these discounts. The hard part was never the law, it was knowing you qualify. WindCredit makes that take two minutes, and it's free."

### YOU-4: Close (2:35–2:50, about 35 words)

> "Next, I want to add Spanish and Haitian Creole and cover every county in Florida. I used AI assistance while building WindCredit, and it's fully disclosed in my submission. Thank you, Representative **[Frankel/Moskowitz]**, for running the Congressional App Challenge."

Then cut to `G3_end.png` for 3 seconds. Edit your name, school and district onto it first, or overlay them as text in your editor.

## Filming checklist
- [ ] Phone horizontal (16:9), at eye level, tripod or leaned on something steady
- [ ] Face lit from the front (face the sun or a window), quiet spot, no wind on the mic
- [ ] Do each face clip 2–3 times and keep the best one
- [ ] Look at the lens, not the screen
- [ ] Speak a little slower than feels natural

## Editing (CapCut or iMovie, about 30 minutes)
1. Drop in clips in timeline order. `VideoClips/WindCredit-demo-footage-16x9.mp4` is all the app footage already joined in order, if you'd rather cut one file.
2. Lay the VO under the footage and trim footage to match the words.
3. Add the captions from the table, big, bottom third, on screen 2+ seconds each.
4. Low background music (YouTube Audio Library is free and copyright-safe), about 15% volume.
5. Export 1080p. Check it's **under 3:00**. Watch it once on mute.
